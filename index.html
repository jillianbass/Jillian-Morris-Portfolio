Jillian Morris is a multidisciplinary designer and art director based in Brooklyn.

She is currently a senior designer at the Metropolitan Opera where she has been a member of the in-house studio since 2017. Her recent work at the Met has included advertising, print, illustration, exhibitions and other special projects.

Jillian has previously worked at agencies in New York and Boston. Her work has encompassed a wide range of clients in various fields from higher education to hospitality.
